# Starter Guide

New to NCL or just looking for tips? Start here!

## General Tips

- **Take notes** - Not only will it be helpful for you to document what works and what doesn't, if you contribute to our guide, you will receive author credit (hint: put it on your resume)!
- **Find a setup that works for you** - Check [this page](guides/lab-guide.md) for advice on setting up your lab.

## Gym

- **You don't need to complete categories in order** - In fact, you probably shouldn't! If you aren't sure where to start, try easy or medium challenges in every category.  This should give you an idea of where to focus your time.
- **Read the gym guides** - It's not 'cheating' - this is how you learn how the challenges work, and what tools might be expected. 

## Practice Game

- **Set periodic goals** - Set smaller goals and increase as you achieve them. This will give you a good sense of what you can do without feeling overwhelmed. Can you get an easy challenge in two categories? What about four? Can you get a category to 50%? What about 80%? 
- **Research** - The gym is great guidance for how many of the challenges work, but it doesn't cover everything. 
- **Advanced tools are probably unnecessary** - This doesn't mean they aren't helpful! It just means *most* challenges emphasize a combination of puzzle-solving skills and basic tools. It's helpful to keep that in mind so you don't get stuck feeling like you don't know enough about hacking tools to progress!

## Competition

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Rank is determined by:
> `Points > Accuracy > Time of Last Correct Submission`
> This means you can approach challenges in this order:
> - Prioritize fast, easy points - If you think you can submit correct answers in a challenge fast and accurately, get those points first!
> - Prioritize what you can get correct - If you think you are likely to submit a correct solution on the first try, get those points next!
> - Get as many points as possible - If you aren't sure about the first two options, just get as many points as you can!
